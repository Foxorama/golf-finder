Right-click-drag pans the map (left click is for drawing). Polygons: click each corner, then Finish (or click the first point). Tee/green-pt = single click. Bunkers & water: place several per hole. Edit/drag mode: drag any placed marker or vertex to reposition (toggle off to add new features). Auto-build: draw the green + place a tee → a straight centreline appears with 100/150/200 markers derived along it. For a dogleg, Edit-mode-drag the centreline (pull its midpoint to the corner) — the markers re-place along the bent line, always on the fairway. Moving a tee/green snaps the line's end. Tee m: type each tee's card metres + place tees to drop them by distance along the line.
