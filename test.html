On-course play & wind

The GPS rangefinder, hole maps & shot-tracking need a phone on a course. Tune via ?playshead=/?playstail=. The wind chips drive the full-screen compass (🧭 day header) via ?wind=spd,dir,gust,twist,spread / setWind(). The compass is coloured by what the wind does to the line you're pointing down — teal helping, amber across, red into your face — which normally needs a phone's compass, so the aim chips force that line via ?aim=/setAim() and are relative to the wind chip you picked. window._windAimCol=false reverts to colouring by strength. Colour is also earned: below the impact floor the whole view stays neutral slate, so a wind that changes nothing looks like nothing — tune where that starts with ?impact=floor,full/setImpact().
